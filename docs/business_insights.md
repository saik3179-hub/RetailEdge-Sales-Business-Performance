# Business Insights — RetailEdge Analytics

## Project Context

The following insights were derived from the synthetic RetailEdge sales dataset covering January 2024 to December 2025.

All figures are fictional and intended for portfolio demonstration.

## 1. Overall Business Performance

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Net Revenue | Approximately ₹7.40 crore |
| Total Cost | Approximately ₹5.66 crore |
| Total Profit | Approximately ₹1.74 crore |
| Profit Margin | 23.56% |
| Delivered Orders | 4,200 |
| Delivered Customers | 485 |
| Average Order Value | ₹17,622 |
| Target Achievement | 97.71% |

## 2. Key Observations

### Revenue Growth
Revenue increased by approximately 9.96% from 2024 to 2025, indicating positive year-over-year growth.

### Regional Performance
The West region was the leading revenue-generating region and also generated the highest profit.

North and West exceeded their annual revenue targets.

### Product Category Contribution
Electronics contributed approximately 39.37% of total revenue, making it the largest category.

Furniture contributed approximately 25.42% of revenue.

Accessories contributed approximately 8.97% of revenue.

### Product Performance
The 43-inch 4K Smart TV was the leading product in the displayed revenue and profit rankings.

### Discount Impact
Total discounts amounted to approximately ₹52.6 lakh. Discounting should be monitored alongside revenue and profit margin to understand its impact on profitability.

### Sales Channel Performance
Direct Sales and Distributor were the leading channels by revenue in the dashboard analysis.

### Target Achievement
Overall target achievement was approximately 97.71%, indicating a shortfall of approximately 2.29% against the total revenue target.

## 3. Management Recommendations

1. Study the practices and product mix contributing to West region's performance.
2. Investigate the reasons for target shortfalls in regions that did not achieve their targets.
3. Monitor Electronics and Furniture category profitability.
4. Evaluate product-level discounting against profit margins.
5. Review the performance and customer contribution of each sales channel.
6. Use monthly target monitoring to identify underperformance earlier.

## 4. Disclaimer

These observations and recommendations relate exclusively to a synthetic portfolio dataset. They do not represent actual commercial results or recommendations for a real organisation.
